# Sura

- Role in the game: flight attendant on the father's jet; secretly an agent.
  Appears at the start of the game, later works against the hero.
- Age: adult (the level-1 prototype sets 20).
- In level 1 (working name "Mira" in the prototype): confident, dry humour,
  wants to move from cabin crew to the cockpit — her cover story. She checks the
  hero from the first minute (she knows his real name from her tablet).
- Look: canon in `../../CHARACTER.md` (SFW part). Her uniform for the island
  flight: light, resort-style, a short fitted dress; not chosen yet.
