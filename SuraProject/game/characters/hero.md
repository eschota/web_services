# The hero

- 18, son of a very rich father; flies alone on the father's business jet.
- Arc of level 1: learns that money does not buy interest. The player can make
  him charming, sincere, awkward or arrogant — his character is not fixed.
- Hook: a message from his father ("We'll talk when you land"), an envelope
  inviting him to an evening event he was not told about, luggage loaded for a
  long stay although he expected a weekend.
- Name and look: open.
