# Design notes

## Format

- Visual novel: stills and short generated video clips, choices, branching
  state (booleans such as trust, curiosity, found clues — no visible
  "seduction meter").
- Interactive exploration of the location (look around the cabin) using camera
  angles from the 3D block-out of the scene.
- A failed approach never hard-stops the level: the plane always lands.

## Content and platforms

- The story, script and storyboards are written PG-13. Scenes beyond PG-13 are
  not described in the script.
- Steam findings (checked 2026-09-30): an "Adult Only Sexual Content" title must
  submit **both the store page and a near-final build** before the Coming Soon
  page is approved; store and library art must be PG-13; screenshots must show
  real gameplay; live-generated adult AI content is not allowed, pre-made AI
  assets are disclosed in the Content Survey; rights to every asset must be
  clear. The Steamworks account currently has no free app credit.
- YouTube (Sura Games channel) gets SFW material only — see `../RULES.md`.
