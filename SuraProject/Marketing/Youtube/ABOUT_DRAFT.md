## Why civitai.red is not linked from YouTube

YouTube's external-links policy removes channels whose descriptions, link
buttons or comments point to sexually explicit material, and civitai.red is an
18+ gallery. One link can take the whole channel down. Traffic goes the other
way: civitai.red posts link to the YouTube devlog and, later, the Steam page.
