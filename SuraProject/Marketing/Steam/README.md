# Steam

Findings of 2026-09-30 (Steamworks partner account checked, Valve docs):

- No free app credit on the Steamworks account — one must be bought first.
- "Adult Only Sexual Content": the store page **and a near-final build** go to
  review together before Coming Soon is approved; review can take longer than
  the usual 3–5 business days.
- Store and library capsules must be PG-13; screenshots must show real
  gameplay (no standalone AI art passed off as screenshots).
- Pre-made AI assets are allowed and disclosed in the Content Survey;
  live-generated adult AI content is not allowed.
- Every asset needs clear distribution rights (check model licences: e.g.
  Qwen-Image 2.1 is non-commercial).
- Coming Soon must be public at least two weeks before release.
