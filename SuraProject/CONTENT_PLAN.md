# Content plan

## Formats (all SFW, vertical 9:16 Shorts, 15–45 s)

1. **"Sura plays"** — Sura reacting over our own game footage (Sand Keeper,
   Little Tilt Constructor). Promotes the games, plays with the gaming category.
2. **"Say Sura hello"** — direct-to-camera greetings, answering a comment.
   Builds the character and comments.
3. **Trend dances / lip-less trends** with Audio Library music.
4. **Behind the AI** — how a Short was made on autorig.online (graph, before /
   after). Also promotes autorig.online.
5. **Outfit / look of the day** — clothed style variations, same face.

## Cadence

- 1 Short per day at a fixed time (18:00 UTC to start), in 3 rotating formats.
- 1 Community post per week (poll: "what game next?").

## First 30 days

| Week | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Branding applied (avatar, banner, description, handle); SFW reference sheet; 5 Shorts in the approval queue |
| 2 | Daily Shorts; test 3 formats; pin a comment asking what to play |
| 3 | Double down on the format with the best average % viewed |
| 4 | First long video (3–5 min: "I made an AI gamer girl"), playlist per format |

## Metrics to watch (Studio, per Short, first 48 h)

- Viewed vs swiped away (> 60 % is good), average % viewed, subscribers per
  1 000 views. Views alone mislead.

## Monetisation path (for reference)

YouTube Partner Program via Shorts: 1 000 subscribers + 10 M Shorts views in
90 days. Check the current thresholds before promising dates.
