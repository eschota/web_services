# Rules for the Sura Games channel

These are hard rules. A video that breaks one gets the channel a strike or a
ban, and a ban also takes every Short and every subscriber with it.

## 1. Nothing sexual reaches YouTube

- No nudity, no implied nudity (covered nipples, towels, bedsheets, "censor"
  bars), no sexual acts or positions, no fetish framing, no suggestive camera on
  the body. YouTube removes sexual content with a synthetic or animated
  character exactly as with a real person.
- Clothing always: a casual or gaming outfit. The explicit tattoos (wings,
  buttock hearts, garters, intimate piercings) are **never shown**; only the
  ones visible in normal clothes (forearm bows, septum, eyes, hair clips).
- A render from the adult pipeline is never re-cut, cropped or blurred into a
  YouTube video. YouTube material is rendered on its own, from SFW prompts.
- Thumbnails follow the same rule; YouTube judges thumbnails more strictly than
  the video.
- No links from YouTube (description, pinned comment, About, banner) to adult
  sites, Civitai NSFW pages, Patreon tiers with adult content, or "link in bio"
  chains leading there.

## 2. Sura is an AI character, and says so

- Tick **"Altered or synthetic content"** on every upload where she looks
  realistic.
- The channel description says she is an AI-generated virtual character.
- She never claims to be a real person, never impersonates a real creator,
  and never uses a real person's face or voice.

## 3. Audience

- Adults and teens watch Shorts. Sura is an adult character (21+) and is
  written as one, but no content plays on youth, school uniforms or
  childlike looks.
- **Made for kids: No** on every upload.

## 4. Music, games, other people's material

- Music only from the YouTube Audio Library or tracks we own. Trending songs
  from TikTok get the Short muted or claimed.
- Game footage: our own games (Sand Keeper, Little Tilt Constructor…) freely;
  other games only as commentary with our own gameplay, never someone else's
  video.

## 5. Publishing discipline

- Every video goes to the owner on https://autorig.online/dev for Принять /
  Улучшить **before** upload. Nothing is published without an explicit accept.
- One upload at a time; no bulk dumps (spam signal).
- Titles and descriptions: no clickbait lies, no keyword stuffing
  (≤ 15 tags, a few hashtags).
- Secrets (OAuth client, refresh tokens) never go into this folder, Git, logs
  or chat. See PIPELINE.md → Upload.
