The plugin is for artists who want the AutoRig workflow inside Blender while keeping the online service available for browser uploads.

1

Open the add-on panel

3

Questions and answers

What is the AutoRig.online Blender plugin?

It is a native Blender add-on with its own panel. It brings the AutoRig automatic rigging workflow into your Blender scene, with presets for humanoid and non-humanoid models.

How many presets does it have?

The plugin has 13 unique presets for humanoid and non-humanoid models, including animals and creatures.

How much does it cost and where do I buy it?

Can I use AutoRig without the plugin?

Yes. The website takes a 3D model, a picture or a picture of a car, opens every task in a 3D viewer with an AI agent, and offers downloads for GLB, FBX, USDZ, ZIP, a Unity package and Blender.
