One picture in, a rigged, animated model out

Most image-to-3D tools stop at a mesh. You get a static model, and rigging it is still your evening. AutoRig treats the picture as the start of the whole conveyor: the picture becomes a 3D model on the AutoRig fleet, the model gets textures, the fast rig builds the skeleton and the skin, animation clips are retargeted at once, and everything opens in one viewer.

The route is decided once, right after upload. A picture of a car goes to the vehicle conveyor, which cuts it into body, wheels and doors instead of rigging it like a character. Any other picture goes through image to 3D model, textures and the rig. Whatever the picture shows, you get a textured PBR 3D model, and the rig is built in the category that fits it: biped, quadruped, equine, bird, serpent, monster, multilimb or hands.

How it works, step by step

Five stages run without you touching anything after the upload.

  1. Drop the picture

    Open the AutoRig home page and drop a PNG, JPG or WEBP file on the upload zone. One image is one task. You need to be signed in, because generation is linked to your account and your plan.

  2. The route is chosen

    A picture of a car goes to the vehicle conveyor. Every other picture goes to image-to-3D generation and then to the rig. Nothing is rejected: the route only decides what is built from the picture.

  3. The 3D model is generated

    The picture goes to the AutoRig fleet and comes back as a 3D model. The free fleet is the default. The paid cloud generators (Tripo, Hunyuan cloud) run only when you press their button.

  4. Textures

    A model's own textures are kept. A model that comes back without them gets PBR textures automatically (albedo, roughness, metallic, normal, emissive), and the textured model is what the vision checks use.

  5. Rig, clips and viewer

    The fast rig builds the skeleton and the skin from the geometry in seconds and fits itself to the model as it is. Clips from the library of the model's category are retargeted at once, and the checks run in the background of the viewer. Everything opens in one viewer with blueprint mode, environment scenes and the session agent chat.

What makes a good picture

The conveyor needs a readable subject, not art quality. A rough sketch with a clear silhouette beats a beautiful render where the limbs are hidden or fused into the body.

  • The whole subject is in frame, including hands, feet, paws or wheels.
  • The subject is seen clearly from the front or at a slight angle, with the limbs visible.
  • Limbs are separated from the body, with a gap between the legs. A T-pose or an A-pose gives the cleanest model, but it is not required.
  • The background is plain, or at least does not blend into the silhouette.
  • One subject per picture.

Animals, birds, hands and creatures

The engine picks the rig category from the model, so you do not choose a template. Quadrupeds, horses and deer, birds, serpents, monsters, multilimb creatures and single hands each have their own skeleton, skin rules and clip library. Wings are rigged as wings, never as arms. See animal rigging and animation retargeting.

A picture of a car

A car is not rigged like a character. The vehicle conveyor starts from your picture and ends as a set of 3D parts that move:

  • Segmentation into the body, wheels, doors, hood, trunk, hatches, glass, lights and bumpers.
  • Every part is isolated and restored as a whole object; the body is drawn as a bare shell.
  • You review the parts in the viewer and accept, redraw or order each one as 3D.
  • Each part becomes its own 3D object. The free fleet is the default; the paid 3D clouds run only on your button.
  • Wheels become cylinder colliders, the body is corrected to symmetry, doors get hinges, and the cabin gets a seat, a steering wheel, pedals, a gear stick and a rigged driver.

Read the vehicle conveyor step by step.

Several pictures at once

Drop a handful of images and each one becomes its own task, queued together. The upload zone accepts a multi-file selection and starts them in sequence, so a set of concept sheets can turn into a set of rigged characters in one pass. Drop a single file and you land on that task's page; drop several and you land on your task list.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need a 3D model to start?

No. That is the point of this page - a single picture is enough. If you already have a GLB, FBX or OBJ, upload it directly instead and skip generation entirely.

Do I need a T-pose picture?

No. A T-pose or an A-pose gives the cleanest model, but the rig is fitted to the generated model as it is, in any pose.

How long does it take?

The 3D model is generated on the AutoRig fleet, so the time depends on the queue and there is no fixed number. The task page shows live progress, and you can close the tab: the task keeps running. Once the model exists, the rig is built from its geometry in seconds and the clips are retargeted at once.

What if the picture is not a character?

A picture of a car goes to the vehicle conveyor. Any other picture still gives a textured 3D model, and it is rigged when it fits one of the rig categories.

Can it rig animals, birds or hands?

Yes. Quadrupeds, equines, birds, serpents, monsters, multilimb creatures and hands each have their own category, next to bipeds and vehicles.

Is the 3D model made by a paid AI service?

No, not by default. It is generated on the AutoRig fleet. The paid cloud generators (Tripo, Hunyuan cloud) run only when you press their button.

Can I rig several characters at once?

Yes. Select several images in the upload zone and each becomes its own task, charged separately.

Do I need an account?

For generating a character from a picture, yes - Unlimited Monthly is linked to your account. Uploading an existing 3D model for rigging remains available without a subscription.
