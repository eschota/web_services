What is OBJ auto rigging?

OBJ (Wavefront OBJ) is a popular format for modelling and sculpting. It carries geometry only, so an OBJ arrives without a skeleton, skin or animation, and its materials live in a separate .mtl file with image files. AutoRig takes it into the V3 conveyor: the skeleton and skin are built in seconds, the clips are retargeted at once and the result opens in the viewer.

Perfect for:

  • ZBrush sculptures
  • Blender models
  • Maya exports
  • 3ds Max models
  • Digital sculpting projects

OBJ rigging features

Geometry preserved

Vertex data, UV mapping and material assignments are kept.

Textures travel with the model

Upload the OBJ with its .mtl and images in a ZIP. An OBJ without its .mtl or images is detected at intake, and you are asked for the files.

PBR textures added

A model without textures gets PBR textures automatically (albedo, roughness, metallic, normal, emissive). The original stays.

Any pose, any category

A category skeleton template is picked from the shape. A T-pose is not required.
