Compositions

Drag from a socket on the right of a node to a socket on the left of another. Only sockets that carry the same kind of thing will connect. Render runs the whole composition; steps that do not depend on each other run at the same time. Select with left-drag or Shift-click. Right-drag pans. Ctrl+C/V copies and pastes; Ctrl+D duplicates; Delete removes selected nodes. Double-click a header for S/M/A views.
