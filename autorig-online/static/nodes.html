Drag a tool from the strip at the top left onto the canvas, or click it to drop a node in the middle of the view. Drag from a socket on the right of a node to a socket on the left of another; only sockets that carry the same kind of thing will connect. Render runs the whole composition; steps that do not depend on each other run at the same time. Select with left-drag or Shift-click. Right-drag pans. Ctrl+C/V copies and pastes; Ctrl+D duplicates; Delete removes selected nodes; Ctrl+P takes them out of the run and puts them back. Shift-click a wire to delete it. Double-click a header for S/M/A views, and Shift-click the S/M/A button to change every node. Right-click a picture node for Functions.
