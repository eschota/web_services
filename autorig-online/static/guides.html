Guides by what you rig

Every category has its own skeleton template, fit rules, skin rules, clip library and quality checks. Start with the one that matches your model.

Characters, heroes and NPCs in any pose. When the feet do not stand side by side, the standing leg sets the lengths for the other leg.

Inside the viewer

Every task is one Unity WebGL viewer with an agent chat. It opens on a black scene at once and plays the rig stages while the model loads.

Edit bones in blueprint mode

Press B to edit bones with symmetry. The model is re-rigged 15 seconds after your last edit, or at once with the button. Confirming the skeleton is offered, never required.

See how it works ->

Camera presets and hotkeys

Jump to the face, elbows, hands, knees, feet or heels. Every control shows its hotkey; right mouse pans and middle mouse orbits.

Read the FAQ ->

The session agent

One shared agent per task works inside the viewer and answers in the language of the person who created the task.

Read the FAQ ->

Review the parts of a car

The Parts panel shows every isolated part. Accept it, redraw it or order it as a 3D model. Paid 3D clouds run only when you press their button.

Environment scenes

Textures and downloads

A model keeps its own textures. One without textures gets PBR textures automatically. Download GLB, FBX, USDZ, ZIP, a Unity package or Blender files.
