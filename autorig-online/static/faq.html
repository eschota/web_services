The viewer

What happens after I upload a model?

Every task opens in its own 3D viewer. The model appears in a lit scene, the auto-rig runs, and the AI agent joins in the corner. Icons at the top show progress; when the rig is ready, the animations play from the bar at the bottom.

How do I move the camera?

Drag with the left mouse button to orbit, with the right button or Shift + left to pan. The wheel changes the lens, Ctrl + wheel moves closer. F frames the model, F11 goes full screen, F12 saves a screenshot and F1 lists every key. On a phone, drag with one finger and pinch with two.

What are the icons around the viewer?

Left: model, rig, bones, joint editor, voxels, framing, auto camera and the outliner. Right: the look of the scene. A click switches an icon to its next preset, the last step turns it off, and holding the icon opens its detailed settings. Bottom left: the channels, keys 1–0. A right click opens a menu with this FAQ.

The AI agent

What can the AI agent do?

The agent lives in your task's viewer. It looks at the model, decides what to do next and uses tools: it checks joints, feet and skinning, repairs frames, adds animations, changes materials, light and scene, and renders images and videos of the result.

How do I talk to the agent?

Type in the chat under the video window in the bottom-right corner, in any language. Ask in plain words: “check the feet”, “make her wave”, “make the eyes glow”. Quick-reply chips suggest next steps, and renders and videos appear right in the chat.

Does the agent see what I see?

It knows which layer and clip are on screen and whether the bones are shown, and it can drive the viewer for you: play a clip, show a layer, move the camera, change materials, weather and time of day.

Will the agent change my original file?

No. Your upload stays as it is. Edits are saved as new versions on top of it, and material changes can be undone.

Rig and skinning

How does auto-rigging work?

AutoRig reads the body plan of the model, places a skeleton inside the mesh, binds the skin to the bones and tests the deformation on real motions. Humanoids get a standard humanoid skeleton that animation tools and game engines understand.

How do I inspect the bones and the skinning?

Press F8 or the bones icon to see the skeleton over the model. Play a clip, press Space to stop on a frame and step with the arrow keys. Channel 0 colours the body parts, and the outliner (9) hides or isolates any part.

A joint is in the wrong place. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the joint editor in T- or A-pose, move or rotate the hand or foot handle and save. The correction goes into every animation, including ones added later. You can also ask the agent to check and repair the feet, the knees or a single frame.

Which models can be rigged?

Humanoid characters work best: people, stylised and anime characters, heroes in armour. Animals and creatures are rigged with skeletons of their own. Upload GLB, FBX or OBJ, or generate a character.

Animation and retargeting

Which animations do I get?

A rigged character comes with ready motions such as idle, walk, run, jump, wave, dance, punch and look around. They play from the bar at the bottom of the viewer, and the agent can add more on request.

What is retargeting?

Retargeting moves a motion made for one skeleton onto yours while keeping your character's proportions. Your joint corrections are carried into every retargeted clip.

Can I fix one bad frame?

Yes. Press Space to stop the clip, find the frame on the slider and press the wrench: the frame is smoothed with its neighbours and flips in the clip are repaired.

Modeling and editing

Can I edit the model itself?

Yes. Pick any object, material or body part in the outliner (9) or click it in the scene, then change its colour, metalness, smoothness and glow. The eye icon hides parts so you can look inside.

Can the agent edit the model for me?

Describe the change and the agent makes it: recolour clothes, make eyes or armour glow, try other materials. Every material change can be undone.

Can I change the scene around the model?

Yes: the post-processing look, depth of field, volumetric light, weather, time of day, stages such as a studio or a cathedral, and a radial pulse on Alt. Each is one click on the right toolbar.

Generation

Can I make a 3D character from a picture?

Yes. Upload an image of a character: AutoRig builds a 3D model from it, rigs it and opens it in the viewer with animations.

Can I describe a character in words?

Yes. Write what you want to see; the character is generated from the description, then rigged and animated the same way.

Can I start from a video?

Yes. Send a video and the agent uses it as a reference for the character and its movement.

Can the agent make images and videos of my character?

Yes: renders, turntables and short videos of the character in a scene. They appear in the chat next to the viewer.

Textures and channels

How do I check textures and materials?

Use the keys 1–0 or the icons in the bottom-left corner: 1 full shading, 2 albedo, 3 normals, 4 metallic, 5 roughness, 6 emission, 7 object IDs, 8 material IDs, 9 the outliner, 0 body parts.

What are object and material IDs?

Each separate object or material gets its own flat colour, so you see at once how the model is split and which parts share a material.

Are my textures kept?

Yes. Materials, textures and UVs are kept through rigging, and the PBR maps (base colour, normal, metal-roughness and emission) show up in the channels.

Export

Which formats can I download?

The rigged, animated character downloads as GLB and FBX. Static copies are available as OBJ and USDZ.

Does it work in Unity, Unreal and Blender?

Yes. The FBX imports as a humanoid in Unity and as a skeletal mesh in Unreal, and the GLB opens in Blender, three.js and other web viewers.

Can I use the result commercially?

Yes, if you own the rights to the source model. AutoRig adds the skeleton, skinning and animations and claims no ownership of your model.

Is there an API?

Yes. Tasks can be created, tracked and downloaded through the API; the developer page has the details.
