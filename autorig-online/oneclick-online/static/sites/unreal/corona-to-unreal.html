Corona Workflow

Keep Corona scene intent while moving to Unreal Engine 5

Corona projects are usually approved because of atmosphere and material subtlety. A successful conversion preserves this perception while making the project responsive for realtime review and walkthrough interaction.

Three practical priorities

Mood continuity

Validate key lighting zones first so clients recognize the same emotional tone as in source previews.

Material behavior

Focus on wood, stone, fabrics, and glazing where differences in roughness response are most visible.

Runtime clarity

Check movement smoothness and visibility transitions in the same camera routes used for presentations.

Delivery context

Teams using Corona often need high-trust visual communication with non-technical stakeholders. Realtime UE5 output helps discussions happen inside the space, not in static screenshot loops.
