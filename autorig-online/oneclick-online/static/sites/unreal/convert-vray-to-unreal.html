V-Ray Transfer Strategy

Convert V-Ray scenes to Unreal Engine 5 with predictable quality

V-Ray scenes often contain subtle gloss, layered material logic, and carefully balanced lighting. The key to reliable conversion is preserving visual intent while adapting for realtime constraints and client interaction.

What to lock before conversion

Material grouping

Define hero materials and secondary surfaces before upload to reduce chaotic remapping later.

Texture consistency

Use deterministic paths and naming to avoid missing maps and cross-scene conflicts.

Viewpoint priorities

Mark key cameras for QA so Unreal validation follows business-critical presentation zones.

After import into Unreal

Start with reflective materials, glass behavior, and primary light contrast. These areas reveal conversion mismatches first and give the fastest confidence signal for production teams.

Then validate hierarchy readability, scene navigation flow, and frame stability in your target hardware profile.
