VR Delivery Path

From 3ds Max scene to comfortable Unreal VR walkthrough

VR presentations are unforgiving: unstable frame pacing, hard camera transitions, and cluttered spatial flow break trust immediately. A strong conversion workflow treats comfort and readability as first-class quality metrics.

Comfort and performance checklist

Route planning

Define intro and hero viewpoints before testing free movement so first-time users stay oriented.

Performance envelope

Lock target hardware profile and tune quality within that budget instead of pushing effects blindly.

Interaction clarity

Keep focal points readable and avoid over-dense detail where users need navigational confidence.

Use-case examples

Residential sales presentations prioritize natural route flow and atmosphere consistency. Corporate spaces often require stronger wayfinding logic and material identity retention. Retail projects benefit from focused hero-zone fidelity and controlled navigation pacing.
